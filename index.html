Our Story

A small café with a serious obsession.

Hendrick Café began in 2019 as a two-person roastery on Kloof Street. We wanted to build a place where the coffee was exceptional, the welcome was warm, and the pace was a little slower than the city outside.

Today we roast every bean ourselves in small batches, source through ethical South African importers, and train every barista to taste — not just pour. No shortcuts, no burnt espresso, no pre-ground bags sitting on a shelf.
